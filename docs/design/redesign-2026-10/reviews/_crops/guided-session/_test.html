Guided Session

The app is your coach. It reads the plan to you, leads you step by step, and tells you what changed and why.
Navigation · No tab bar. Today is the hub page; History, Progress, Program, Exercises and Settings are pushed pages with a back chevron. Workout mode is a full-screen stream; Pause returns to Today, where a Resume card takes the single primary.
Logging · A guided step stream on a 2 px rail: confirm with one tap (Done · 62.5 × 8) or adjust in a bottom sheet. Warm-ups, rest and PRs are written into the stream as steps; rest auto-advances to the next set.
Look · Light, editorial: navy ink on a cool off-white, one rust accent for action, focus and PR. Newsreader for at most two headlines or coach lines per screen, never numbers; IBM Plex Sans tabular for all UI and figures. Radii 4 / 6 / 8 / 12.
